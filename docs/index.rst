How to Login Using the iPhone Mail App
======================================

The built-in Mail app on the iPhone provides an easy way to access your MSN Email account.

Open the **Settings** application on your iPhone.

Select **Mail**.

Choose **Accounts**.

Tap **Add Account**.

Select the option for Microsoft accounts if available.

Enter your MSN Email address.

Tap **Next**.

Enter your password carefully.

Complete any security verification if prompted.

Allow the Mail app to synchronize your emails, contacts, calendars, and other available data.

Once setup is complete, open the Mail app to access your MSN Email inbox.

How to Login Using a Web Browser
================================

You can also access MSN Email through Safari or any other supported browser installed on your iPhone.

Open your preferred browser.

Visit the official Microsoft sign-in page.

Enter your MSN Email address.

Tap **Next**.

Enter your password.

Complete any required verification.

After successful authentication, your inbox will open and you can begin managing your emails.

Using a browser is a convenient option if you do not wish to add your account permanently to your device.
